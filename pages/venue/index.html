Central Park, Hulhumalé

Three days in the open air. One park, four zones, and everything you need to find in about ninety seconds.

Dates

6–8 November 2026 Friday to Sunday

Daily hours

10:00 – 22:00 Last entry 21:30

Entry

Free for everyone Registration recommended, not required

Where

Central Park, Hulhumalé 15 min from Malé · 10 min from the airport

The site

Four zones on one loop

One circular path runs through the whole park. Start at any entrance, walk the loop once, and you will have passed every zone.

Getting there

  • Ferry from Malé north harbour, every 15 minutes, roughly 15 minutes door to door.
  • Bus across the Sinamalé Bridge, every 10–20 minutes until late.
  • Taxi from Malé over the bridge, about 15 minutes; 10 minutes from Velana International Airport.
  • Parking on the streets bordering the park — busiest after 18:00.

Accessibility

  • Step-free throughout — every path, stand and stage is on one level.
  • Accessible toilets at the main entrance and beside the stage.
  • Assistance on request — ask any steward, or call ahead of your visit.
  • Quiet, shaded seating away from the stage for anyone who needs a break.

On site

  • Prayer facilities for men and women, with wudhu.
  • First aid staffed by the Maldivian Red Crescent, all opening hours.
  • Food and drink stalls, plus free drinking water points.
  • Lost property at the main entrance until 22:30 each day.

Plan your visit

Quietest — best for talking to exhibitors10:00 – 13:00
Steady, full programme running13:00 – 17:00
Busiest — stage sets and families18:00 – 22:00

Come in the morning if you want time with employers. Come in the evening if you want the atmosphere.

What to bring

  • Several printed copies of your CV.
  • Photo ID — required to check in at the Interview Lounge.
  • A hat, sunscreen and a refillable bottle. It is an open park.
  • Families: buggy-friendly paths, baby change at the main toilets.
