What produced them

Three things the floor does differently.

Employers come to hire, not to advertise.

Stands are staffed by people who can make an offer. In 2025 shortlists were drawn during the expo and apprenticeship agreements were signed and countersigned on the floor.

The money questions get answered on site.

Scholarship and student-loan officers from the Ministry sit beside the institutions, so a visitor can ask what a course costs and what support exists in the same conversation.

The labour market is discussed in public.

Afternoon panels put employers, training providers and graduates on the same stage to talk about wages, localisation and the skills the economy is short of.

Who runs it

Convened by the Ministry.

VARA Expo is run by the Ministry of Homeland Security, Labour and Technology, which is why the numbers above are not only attendance figures. The scholarship desk, the student-loan officers and the apprenticeship registry are on the floor, so a visit converts into a place, a loan or a contract rather than a leaflet.

The 2026 edition runs 6–8 November at Central Park, Hulhumalé. Free entry, no registration at the gate.
