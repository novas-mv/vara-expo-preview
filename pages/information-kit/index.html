Key dates

The 2026 timetable

Three of these are hard deadlines: the application close, the payment date and the forms date. Miss one and your stand goes back on the plan.

  1. 12 Jan 2026

    Applications open

    The online application and the prospectus go live. Early applications get first pick of stand position.

  2. 29 May 2026

    Application deadline

    Applications close at 15:00. Late requests join a waiting list and are only placed if a stand is released.

  3. 19 Jun 2026

    Stand allocation confirmed

    You receive your zone, stand number and neighbouring exhibitors, with the floor plan marked up.

  4. 14 Aug 2026

    Payment deadline

    The balance is due in full. Unpaid stands are released back to the waiting list the following week.

  5. 25 Sep 2026

    Forms deadline

    Power and water requests, insurance certificate, risk assessment and your directory listing copy.

  6. 4–5 Nov 2026

    Build-up days

    Hall access 08:00–20:00 both days. All stands must be dressed and cleared by 20:00 on 5 November.

  7. 6–8 Nov 2026

    Show days

    Open to the public 09:00–18:00. Exhibitor access from 08:00.

  8. 8–9 Nov 2026

    Breakdown

    From 18:30 on 8 November until 14:00 on 9 November. Nothing may be dismantled before the hall closes.

What you get

What a stand includes

Included in the stand fee

  • Shell scheme 3m × 2m walled bay, 2.5m high, with a printed fascia carrying your name.
  • Power One 13A socket and a 500W allowance. Three-phase is orderable separately.
  • Lighting Two LED spots on the fascia rail, on the hall circuit for all three days.
  • Furniture One counter, two stools and a waste bin, delivered before build-up closes.
  • Wifi Exhibitor SSID with four device credentials per stand. Wired lines are orderable.
  • Directory listing Your name, zone, stand number and 60-word profile in the printed and online directory.

Orderable at extra cost

  • Extra power Additional sockets, three-phase supply and 24-hour circuits.
  • Plumbed water A water point and waste drain — required for any food preparation.
  • Custom build Space-only plots from 12m² if you are bringing your own structure.
  • Rigging & banners Anything suspended from the roof structure must be ordered and certified.
  • Storage Locked overnight storage in the service corridor, by the pallet.
  • Extra badges Six exhibitor badges are included; more are available at cost.

Required reading

Rules & requirements

The full text is in the Health & Safety Policy. This is the short version.

Insurance

Every exhibitor must hold public liability cover of at least MVR 5 million for the full period from build-up to breakdown, naming VARA Expo as an interested party. A copy of the certificate is due with your forms on 25 September 2026. Stands without a certificate on file are not released on build-up day.

Health & safety

You are responsible for your own stand, your staff and your contractors. A short risk assessment is required for every stand and a full method statement for any space-only build. Gangways must stay clear, and nothing may project beyond your stand line.

Live demonstrations

Welding, cutting, hot work, machinery and anything with a moving part must be declared in advance and screened from the public. The Skills Experience zone is built for this; demonstrations elsewhere are assessed case by case. A fire extinguisher and a trained operator are required at all times.

Food handling

Any stand preparing, sampling or selling food needs a Maldives Food and Drug Authority permit, a plumbed water point and a designated handwash station. Cooking with open flame is permitted only in the Market zone service bays.

Noise

Sound from your stand must stay under 75 dB measured at the gangway. Amplified music, PA systems and demonstration sirens are limited to scheduled slots so that neighbouring stands can hold a conversation.

Waste

Take away what you bring in. General and recycling bins are provided at the end of each aisle; packaging, offcuts and build waste are your own to remove. Anything left after 14:00 on 9 November is cleared at the exhibitor's cost.

Exhibitor FAQ

Questions we get every year

How much does a stand cost?

Rates for 2026 are set out in the prospectus and vary by zone and stand size. Small businesses and registered training providers qualify for a reduced rate, and there is a limited allocation of subsidised stands for first-time exhibitors in the Market zone.

Can we choose our own stand position?

You can state a preference for a zone and for a position on the plan when you apply. Allocation is confirmed on 19 June and balances your preference against zone mix, stand size and how early you applied.

What is the difference between shell scheme and space only?

Shell scheme is the walled bay described above, ready to dress. Space only is a marked floor plot from 12m² where you bring your own structure — it needs a method statement and a longer build-up slot.

Do we need to staff the stand for all three days?

Yes. Stands must be staffed from 09:00 to 18:00 on each of the three show days. Leaving a stand unattended during opening hours affects your allocation next year.

Can we share a stand with another organisation?

Two organisations may share a stand if both are named on the application. Both appear in the directory. Sub-letting a stand to an organisation not named on the application is not permitted.

What happens if we need to cancel?

Cancellations before the payment deadline forfeit the deposit only. After 14 August the full fee stands, though we will try to re-let the stand and refund what we recover.

Is there a deadline for the directory listing?

Your 60-word profile and logo are due with the rest of your forms on 25 September 2026. After that the printed directory goes to press and only the online listing can be changed.

Who do we contact during build-up?

The exhibitor services desk sits at the north entrance and is staffed from 08:00 on both build-up days and from 07:30 on each show day. Phone and email details are in the build-up schedule.

Ready to take a stand?

Applications close 29 May 2026. If something here is unclear, ask before you apply — it is much easier to change a zone now than after allocation.
