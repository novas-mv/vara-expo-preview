How it works

Five steps, start to finish

You do not need experience, a contact inside the company, or a perfect CV. You need a printed CV, your ID, and a couple of hours.

  1. 01

    Book a slot — or plan to walk in

    Pick an employer, a day and a time online, and you will be held a named place. If you would rather not commit, half of every day is reserved for walk-ins; arrive between 10:00 and 12:00 for the shortest queue.

  2. 02

    Prepare before you arrive

    Print three copies of your CV, gather your certificates and bring photo ID. If your CV needs work, come to the free CV clinic first — it runs continuously and takes about twenty minutes.

  3. 03

    Check in at the lounge desk

    Give your name or booking reference at the Career zone entrance. You will be asked whether you want to be interviewed in Dhivehi or English, and handed a number.

  4. 04

    Sit down and interview

    A real interview with a real hiring manager, typically fifteen to twenty-five minutes. You keep your CV copy; they keep theirs. Nervous? Do a free mock interview at the practice desk beforehand.

  5. 05

    Leave with an outcome

    Most employers follow up within two weeks. Some decide on the day — in 2025 candidates left the hall with written offers, and apprenticeship placements were signed on site.

Before you come

What to bring

Check-in is quicker, and the interview goes better, when you have these six things with you.

  • Three printed CV copies One per interview plus a spare for the clinic. A4, no folder needed.
  • Photo ID National ID card or passport. Check-in cannot register you without it.
  • Certificates & transcripts Originals or clear copies of your highest qualification and any trade certificates.
  • Your booking reference On your phone or printed. Walk-ins are issued a queue number instead.
  • Interview-appropriate clothing Smart, comfortable, and suited to a full day on your feet.
  • Something to write with You will be given contacts and next steps to note down.

Booking

Book a slot, or walk in

Pre-booked

Choose an employer, a day and a time in advance. You are held a named place, you skip the queue, and the employer sees your CV before you sit down. Slots open eight weeks before the expo and the popular ones go first.

Book a slot

Walk-in

About half of every day is kept open for people who did not book. Take a number at the lounge desk and you will be called for the next free interviewer. Queues are shortest between 10:00 and 12:00.

Questions

Before you ask

Do I need to book, or can I just walk in?

Both work. Roughly half of every day is held open for walk-ins, and the rest is pre-booked online. Booking guarantees you a named employer and a time; walking in means queueing, which is shortest between 10:00 and 12:00.

What language will the interview be in?

Sessions run in Dhivehi and English. Tell the check-in desk which you prefer and we match you to an interviewer accordingly. Choosing Dhivehi does not narrow the roles open to you.

Does the CV clinic cost anything?

No. The CV clinic and the mock-interview desk are free for every visitor, staffed by practising HR professionals, and run continuously from 10:00 to 22:00 on all three days.

Can I really be hired on the day?

Yes. At the 2025 edition several candidates received written offers before leaving the hall, and a number of apprenticeship placements were signed on site. Most employers follow up within two weeks instead.

How many employers can I see in one day?

Two interviews plus a CV clinic visit is a realistic day. You may book one slot per employer, up to three across the three days.

What if I have never interviewed before?

Start at the mock-interview desk. It is a twenty-minute practice run with feedback, and no employer sees it. Most first-time candidates do this before their real slot.

Ready when you are

Booking takes two minutes and costs nothing. If you would rather decide on the day, just turn up — the lounge is open from 10:00.
