VARA Expo Wraps Up with Over 20,000 Visitors, Surpassing Expectations

The expo concluded on Saturday night at Central Park, Hulhumalé, after three days of career, education, skills and market programming.

09 Nov 2025 Ministry of Homeland Security, Labour and Technology 4 min read

VARA Expo 2025 closed on Saturday night at Central Park, Hulhumalé, drawing more than 20,000 visitors across three days and comfortably overshooting the 15,000 the organisers had planned for.

The final attendance figure was confirmed by the Ministry of Homeland Security, Labour and Technology on Sunday morning. Gate counts across the three days rose steadily, with Saturday accounting for close to half of the total as families arrived through the late afternoon and stayed into the evening programme.

More than 150 exhibitors took part, distributed across the expo's four zones — Career, Education, Skills Experience and Market. Employers, training providers, universities and small enterprises each held a distinct part of the ground, and organisers said the zoning made it easier for visitors to move with a purpose rather than wander a single undifferentiated hall.

Inauguration and programme

The expo was inaugurated on Thursday evening by the First Lady, Sajidha Mohamed, who toured the four zones and spoke with exhibitors and students. Over the following two days the main stage carried a run of panel discussions on the Maldivian labour market, covering skills shortages in construction and tourism, the pipeline from secondary school into technical training, and the position of women returning to work.

What the four zones covered

  • Career — employers recruiting directly, with live interview booths.
  • Education — local and overseas institutions, scholarship and admissions desks.
  • Skills Experience — hands-on demonstrations from technical and vocational providers.
  • Market — small enterprises and self-employment support.

The live interview booths in the Career zone proved to be the most heavily used part of the ground. Employers ran scheduled and walk-in interviews through all three days, and several made offers on the spot rather than carrying candidates into a later round.

“We set out to make this a place where something actually happens — not a place where you collect brochures. People left with offers in hand.”

Spokesperson, Ministry of Homeland Security, Labour and Technology

Placements and outcomes

Apprenticeship placements were signed on site, with training providers and employers completing agreements at the booths rather than following up afterwards. The Ministry said final figures on offers and placements would be published once exhibitors have returned their reporting forms.

  1. Register interest at the booth and complete a short screening.
  2. Interview in one of the live booths in the Career zone.
  3. Sign the apprenticeship or offer letter on site where both parties agree.

The next edition of VARA Expo is scheduled for 6–8 November 2026, again at Central Park, Hulhumalé. Exhibitor registration is expected to open in the first half of the year, and the Ministry has said it will expand the Skills Experience zone after visitor demand outstripped the floor space allocated to it.
