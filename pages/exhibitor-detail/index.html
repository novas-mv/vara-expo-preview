About Bank of Maldives

Bank of Maldives is the country’s largest bank and, for most Maldivians, the first financial institution they ever deal with. It operates a branch or service centre in every atoll, which makes it one of the very few employers in the country whose career paths genuinely run outside Malé.

That reach is the reason the bank staffs so broadly. A retail network spread across the archipelago needs customer service officers, tellers, relationship managers and branch leadership in places where the nearest colleague may be an hour by ferry. It also needs an unusually capable technology function, because the alternative to a branch visit is an app that has to work on a slow connection at the far end of the country.

The bank runs a structured Graduate Trainee Programme that rotates new joiners through retail, operations, risk and technology over eighteen months before they settle into a function. Alongside it sits a shorter internship intake aimed at students still in their final year, and a technology apprenticeship for people coming in without a computing degree.

At VARA Expo the bank is treating the three days as a live hiring round rather than a brand exercise. Recruiters are on the stand for the full opening hours, and shortlisted candidates are taken straight through to the Interview Lounge for a first-round conversation the same day.
