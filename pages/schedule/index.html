02Plan your day

Four zones and the Interview Lounge, all open 10:00 to 22:00. Sessions are drop-in unless the card says to register at a desk first.

Career

Panels, employer talks and speed networking. Same-day shortlisting through the Lounge.

Education

Scholarship and student-loan desks, study-abroad clinics, TVET pathway sessions.

Skills Experience

Live competitions and walk-up simulators — dive, aviation, hospitality, robotics, drones.

Market

Island enterprise stalls and the evening programme, running late all three nights.

→Coming on the day?

Entry is free. Registering gets you a printed pass, a session reminder and priority in the Interview Lounge queue.
