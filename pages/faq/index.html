Visiting

Entry, hours, getting there, facilities

Is entry free?

Yes. Entry is free for everyone on all three days, and no ticket is needed at the gate. Every talk, demonstration and clinic inside the park is included at no cost.

Do I need to register before I come?

No — you can walk in. Registering in advance simply saves you time on the day:

  • A printed name badge waiting at the arrivals desk
  • A pre-booked Interview Lounge slot rather than a walk-in queue
  • The daily programme sent to you the evening before

You can register in about two minutes and change or cancel it later.

What are the opening hours?

The park is open 10:00–22:00 daily across all three days. Stands are staffed throughout; last entry is 21:30 and the main stage programme ends at 21:00.

Where exactly is the expo held?

Central Park, Hulhumalé. The main entrance is on the west side of the park, facing the Highway. Arrivals, the information desk and the badge collection point are all within the first twenty metres. See the venue page for a site map.

How do I get there, and is there parking?

From Malé, ferries run every 15 minutes until 23:00, with extra sailings on the closing night. Buses stop on the Highway, a four-minute walk from the west gate.

Free motorcycle and car parking is marked on the north side of the park. It fills by early evening, so the ferry is the faster option after 18:00.

Is it suitable for families and younger children?

Yes. Parents are welcome and encouraged — a large share of visitors come as families. There is a supervised activity area for under-12s beside the food court, and the whole route is pushchair accessible.

What accessibility support is available?

All main routes are step-free and firm underfoot. Accessible toilets are at three points on the site, wheelchairs can be borrowed free from the information desk, and main stage sessions are sign-language interpreted. Tell us what you need when you register and a steward will meet you at the gate.

Are there prayer facilities and food on site?

Prayer rooms with ablution facilities, separate for men and women, are at the east and west ends of the park. Stand activity pauses during congregational prayer times.

The food court runs the full opening hours with hot meals and short eats, all halal, and there are free drinking-water refill points at each zone entrance.

Students & job seekers

CVs, interviews, scholarships

What should I bring with me?

Come prepared to hand something over. We suggest:

  • Five to ten printed copies of your CV
  • Your national ID card — needed for the Interview Lounge
  • Photocopies of certificates and results slips
  • A pen and something to write on

If you would rather send documents digitally, every stand has a QR code that opens its contact form.

Can I actually interview on the day?

Yes. The Interview Lounge holds real first-round interviews throughout the three days. Slots can be pre-booked when you register, and walk-in slots are released every hour at the lounge desk. Bring your ID and a CV copy.

Are scholarships available, and can I check eligibility there?

Yes. Ministry scholarship officers sit in the Education zone and run same-day eligibility checks. Bring your results slips and ID and you will leave knowing which schemes you qualify for and when they next open.

Is there help with writing a CV?

The CV clinic is free and runs all three days in 20-minute sessions. Sign up at the clinic desk on arrival. If you have no CV yet, bring your details and an adviser will build a first draft with you and print it.

What ages is the expo for?

The programme is aimed at ages 16 and above — school leavers, students and anyone changing career. Nobody is turned away on age, and the activity area keeps younger family members occupied while you visit stands.

Exhibitors

Stands, costs, build and breakdown

How do I apply for a stand?

Apply through the online exhibitor form, which asks for your organisation details, the zone you want and what you plan to show. Applications are reviewed weekly and you will have a decision within ten working days. Start from the exhibitor information kit.

What does a standard stand include?

A standard stand is 3m × 2m of shell scheme and comes with:

  • Two 13A power outlets and overhead lighting
  • A fascia board printed with your organisation name
  • One table and two chairs
  • Wired water supply on request, in the two service rows only
  • Shared event wi-fi and overnight security

Anything beyond that — extra power, screens, custom build — is ordered through the appointed contractor.

What does it cost, and when are the deadlines?

A standard stand is MVR 7,500 for all three days; corner and double stands are priced in the information kit. Registered training providers and NGOs pay a reduced rate.

Applications close 15 January 2026. Payment is due within 14 days of confirmation, and cancellations are refundable in full up to 1 February 2026.

Can we run a live demonstration?

Yes, and we encourage it — working demonstrations draw the biggest crowds. Submit the safety form at least 21 days before the event if your demonstration involves machinery, heat, water or anything above head height. The operations team will confirm placement and any barriers needed.

When can we set up and break down?

Build-up is the day before opening, 08:00–20:00, with vehicle access until 17:00. Breakdown starts at 22:30 on the final day and the site must be clear by 02:00. Nothing may be dismantled while the park is still open to the public.

Who do we contact about our stand?

The exhibitor team answers stand, build and logistics questions on exhibitors@varaexpo.mv, weekdays 09:00–15:00. During build-up and the event itself, the operations desk at the west gate is staffed from 07:00.

Media & partners

Accreditation, filming, sponsorship

How do I get press accreditation?

Apply at least ten days before opening with your outlet name, editor contact and ID. Approved passes are collected from the media desk at the west gate and give access to the press room, the stage pit and interview slots with organisers.

Can we film or photograph inside the park?

Accredited media may film anywhere in the public areas. Ask permission before filming inside a stand or in the Interview Lounge, where conversations are private. Drone filming needs a separate permit applied for at least 14 days ahead.

How do we discuss sponsorship?

Partnership packages run from zone sponsorship to the main stage and the Interview Lounge. Write to partners@varaexpo.mv with your organisation and objectives and the team will send the current pack, or see the sponsors page for who is already on board.
